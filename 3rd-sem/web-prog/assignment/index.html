King's Book of Glory

Game rules

  • Every house must own exactly as many connected cells as the number on it (the house cell counts too).
  • Property cells connect horizontally and vertically — diagonal contact does not count.
  • A property may contain exactly one house.
  • Two different properties may never touch horizontally or vertically — walls must separate them. They may touch diagonally.
  • All wall cells must form a single connected network.
  • Walls may never fill a whole 2×2 block.

Controls

  • Left-click — build / remove a wall. Hold and drag to paint several cells.
  • Right-click — mark a cell as wall-free property (or remove the mark).
  • Cells with houses cannot be changed.
